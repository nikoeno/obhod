# direct, desync (DPI evasion) or tunnel. Domains that work fine stay direct.

# A domain also covers its subdomains. Edit freely: add your own, remove what
# you do not need. Changes apply the next time splitter starts.
# A domain listed in proxy.txt or block.txt is taken out of this list.

# by DPI (TSPU) in Russia. Being listed here is no claim that they are blocked
